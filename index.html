QUESTIONS
Is it free?

Yes. No account, no key, no trial. Ko-fi is there if you want to help.

Can I get banned?

It can happen: editing the client's memory is against the Roblox Terms of Use. Use accounts you are ready to risk.

Does it work with Bloxstrap or Fishstrap?

Yes. Their players are found, and their exported flag files load as they are.

Why are some flags ignored?

Your Roblox version does not have them anymore. Prism skips them instead of guessing.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Prism > Uninstall.
